                📑 DR / Delivery Dates

                One column per DR: type the DR No. (or invoice no.) and the actual Delivery Date, then the qty of each item that came on that DR. An item that came on two dates gets a qty in two columns. The SOP uses these dates for the days late and the penalty. Not printed on the IAR.
